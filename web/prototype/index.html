Questions people ask

Can I get it back?

Nope.Nobody can. There's no owner, no withdraw function and no upgrade path. Once it's deployed, that's it.

Why not just send stuff to 0x…dEaD?

A dead address has no code. It logs nothing, and NFTs sent with safeTransferFrom bounce right off it. The can accepts every standard and logs an event for each proper burn.

How do I feed it ERC20 tokens?

Approve it, then call burnERC20(token, amount). It pulls the tokens and logs how many actually arrived.

I used transfer() and there's no event?

Your tokens are still gone. A plain transfer() or NFT transferFrom just doesn't tell the can, so there's no receipt.

Can I trust the burn events?

Not blindly. The NFT hooks are public, and ERC20Deposited can be faked with a bogus balanceOf. Cross-check the token's own Transfer event.

Why does the event show less than I sent?

Fee-on-transfer tokens take a cut on the way in. The can logs what actually arrived.

Some tokens won't go in. Why?

ERC777 tokens that enforce hooks revert, since the can isn't registered with ERC-1820. USDC-style tokens can also blocklist the can, and nobody can appeal that.

Is it the same address on every chain?

Yes. It's deployed through the canonical CREATE2 factory, so it lives at the same address on 100+ chains.
